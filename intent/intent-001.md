# Intent: refactor the project to refer to publishing to confluence rather than "sync"

## Problem
The term "sync" implies more than what is going on here.  We are publishing content from an obsidian vault to confluence for broader consumption, but we never consume pages from confluence to be represented in the vault.  The confusion of the terms leads to some bad assumptions.

## Proposed outcome
Code, settings, errors, documentation, all refers to "publish" rather than "sync" when describing this one-way movement of information.
